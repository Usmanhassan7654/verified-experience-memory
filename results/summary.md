## Policies

- `no_memory`: Always ask the source of truth. The accuracy and cost reference.
- `naive`: Remember every answer and every fact or instruction it reads. Typical auto-extracted memory.
- `ttl_7d`: Naive, but entries expire after 7 days. A common quick fix.
- `trust_gated`: Only tool results and trusted staff notes are remembered. No invalidation.
- `version_pinned`: Remember everything, but each entry is pinned to source versions and re-checked before use.
- `verified`: Trust-gated writes, untrusted facts verified against the source before storing, instructions from untrusted text never stored, version check on every read.
